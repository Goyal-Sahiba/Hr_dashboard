# HR Analytics Dashboard | SQL Server & Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee data to understand workforce distribution, employee attrition, job satisfaction, salary trends, overtime, performance, and promotion opportunities.

## Tools & Technologies
- **SQL Server:** Data analysis, aggregation, filtering, CTEs, and window functions.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, charts, slicers, and bookmarks.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and columns for workforce and attrition analysis.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Executive Workforce Overview** – Analyzes employee demographics, department distribution, and salary trends.
2. **Employee Attrition Analysis** – Identifies departments, job roles, experience levels, and workplace factors associated with higher attrition.
3. **Employee Performance & Workforce Strategy** – Explores salary, job satisfaction, work-life balance, performance, and promotion gaps.

## Key Insights
- Identified departments and job roles with higher employee attrition.
- Analyzed the relationship between overtime, job satisfaction, and employee turnover.
- Evaluated salary patterns, employee experience, and promotion opportunities.
- Developed interactive views to help HR identify areas for improvement.

## Project Objective
To transform employee data into meaningful insights that support data-driven HR decisions, employee retention, and workforce plannin
